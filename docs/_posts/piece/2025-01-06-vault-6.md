A PowerVault is a weather-resistant chest that provides for safe electrical usage outside the confines of a sheltering building.  More details about the vault design in general is in [PowerVault Gen-1]({% post_url 2025-01-01-powervault-gen-1 %})

Vault-6 is multiple-MPPT solar collector and pairs with [Vault-4]({% post_url 2025-01-03-vault-4 %}) to store the harvested energy at 48V in 20kWh of batteries.  
 <!--more-->

Vault-6 came into existence after the first pass at Vault-4 completed with space for two MPPTs and 15kWh of storage.  When two more arrays were added, it pushed beyond the Vault-4 design and there was no easy way to add the additional input-current capacity without giving up some of the space within Vault-4s shelving system.

Instead of fiddling with this, having Vault-6 take on all responsibilities for collection improved several things:
* Significant additional space (enough for 4 mppts) was added
* The layout of Vault-6 was much cleaner and more visible than the shelving system
* Additional OCD devices were added to make wire size changes simpler
* Space inside Vault-4 was added allowing an additional 5kWh of storage
* Cooling could be added to the collectors without harming the insulation characteristics of Vault-4 and the batteries

## [System Overview](#system-overview)

Vault-6 is a 48V collector (BESS + solar harvesting) designed for:
   * Multiple MPPTs (four or so)
   * Medium-amperage (200A+) DC-out from the collector
   * Control system space (e.g. a Victron Cerbo)
   * Ability to react to BRB (Big Red Button) pushes by shutting down the TIGO (or similar) rapid-shutdown-devices

It has no abilities beyond the above: it is very focused and the component boundary between it and the BESS & Control Cabinet is very clear.
